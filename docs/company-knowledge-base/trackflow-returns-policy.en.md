# TrackFlow Returns Policy

## Standard return window

The standard return window for eligible products is 30 calendar days from the date of delivery to the end consumer. Returns requested after day 30 are out of policy unless the client's contract includes an extended window documented in writing.

To start a domestic return, the brand or consumer must submit a return request through TrackFlow's returns channel with the original tracking number and reason code. TrackFlow issues a return label within 1 business day for approved domestic requests.

## Domestic return handling

Domestic returns within the same country as the fulfilling warehouse follow the automatic approval path when all of the following are true:

- The request is inside the 30-day window.
- The item is unused, in original packaging, and matches the shipped SKU.
- The product is not marked as final-sale, personalized, or hazardous.

Approved domestic returns are collected by the assigned carrier within 3 business days of label issuance. Refund or restock credit is posted within 5 business days after the warehouse confirms receipt and inspection.

## International returns

International returns (cross-border between the United States and Spain, or returns originating outside the fulfilling country) are **never automatic**. They always require manual handling by the Reverse Logistics team led by Sofía Ramos.

Account managers must not describe international returns as automatic, self-serve, or same-day. Manual review covers eligibility, customs documentation, collection scheduling, and restocking destination. Manual international return decisions are typically completed within 5 to 8 business days after the request is submitted.

## Non-eligible items and restocking fees

The following categories are not eligible for return under the standard policy: opened hygiene products, perishable goods, custom-printed packaging, and items damaged by the consumer after delivery.

A restocking fee of 10% of the product value applies to domestic returns that arrive without original packaging or with missing accessories. Restocking fees for international returns are assessed case by case during manual review and are never waived by account managers without written commercial approval.
