# TrackFlow Carrier Coverage

## United States carriers

TrackFlow works with the following carriers for United States destinations:

- UPS: strongest national coverage for standard and express parcels; preferred for mainland urban and suburban lanes.
- FedEx: strong express and overnight options; preferred when the client requires 1-day express outside same-day metro zones.
- DHL: used primarily for US outbound international and selected dense metro lanes.

Local regional carriers may be assigned for last-mile densification in Los Angeles when volume thresholds are met. Carrier selection for a given shipment considers destination ZIP, weight, and urgency.

## Spain carriers

TrackFlow works with the following carriers for Spain destinations:

- MRW: strong coverage across peninsular Spain, including many secondary cities.
- SEUR: reliable urban and express coverage in major metros.
- DHL: used for Spain outbound international and selected express domestic lanes.

Two local carriers support densification around Zaragoza and selected regional hubs. Rural destinations may require an extra transit day compared with major city SLAs.

## Rural Aragón and secondary zones

For rural Aragón and similar low-density Spanish zones, MRW is the preferred carrier under standard commercial guidance because of broader pickup and delivery density outside metro Zaragoza.

SEUR remains available for urgent urban handoffs in Zaragoza city but is not the default recommendation for dispersed rural Aragón postal codes. Account managers should set expectations that rural Aragón deliveries commonly use the full standard ground window and may require an additional business day versus Madrid or Barcelona.

Carrier exceptions outside this documented coverage matrix (for example forcing FedEx into a Spain-only lane, or promising a carrier that does not serve the ZIP) require commercial approval and must not be invented during a sales call.

## Weight and service limits

Standard parcel coverage assumes packages up to 30 kg for domestic ground services. Shipments above 30 kg or with oversized dimensions are routed as freight-style consignments and may use a different carrier set with longer transit times.

Dangerous goods, lithium batteries above threshold, and temperature-controlled products need pre-clearance before any carrier commitment is given to a prospect.
