# TrackFlow Delivery SLA

## Standard domestic delivery windows

TrackFlow offers the following standard delivery SLAs for domestic shipments within the United States and Spain when inventory is available in the local warehouse:

- Standard ground: delivery within 3 to 5 business days after carrier handoff.
- Express: delivery within 1 to 2 business days after carrier handoff.
- Same-day metro: available only in Los Angeles and Zaragoza metro zones when the order is confirmed before 11:00 local warehouse time; delivery by 21:00 the same day.

Business days exclude weekends and local public holidays in the destination country.

## Cross-border and international SLAs

Shipments between the United States and Spain are treated as international TrackFlow lanes:

- US to Spain standard: 7 to 10 business days after export clearance.
- Spain to US standard: 7 to 10 business days after export clearance.
- Express international: 4 to 6 business days, subject to customs inspection delays that TrackFlow does not control.

Customs holds, incorrect commercial invoices, and restricted-goods screening pause the SLA clock until the shipment is cleared.

## On-time commitment and measurement

TrackFlow measures on-time delivery as the percentage of parcels delivered by the end of the promised SLA window. The commercial standard commitment for annual contracts is an on-time rate of at least 95% for domestic standard ground, calculated monthly per client.

Failed delivery attempts caused by incorrect recipient addresses, refused parcels, or closed businesses are excluded from the on-time calculation. Carrier force-majeure events are also excluded.

## High-demand peak dates warning

During declared high-demand dates — including Black Friday, Cyber Monday, and major seasonal Sales events published in the annual commercial calendar — TrackFlow does **not** guarantee standard delivery SLAs.

Account managers must not promise 3–5 day standard ground or 1–2 day express windows during those periods. Peak-period capacity is best-effort only. Clients should be told that transit times may extend by 2 to 5 additional business days and that published SLAs resume on the first business day after the declared peak window ends.

Any client-specific peak SLA exception requires written approval from Miguel Torres (Commercial Director) before it is offered.
