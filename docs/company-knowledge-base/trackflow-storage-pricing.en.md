# TrackFlow Storage Pricing

## Base monthly storage fees

TrackFlow charges warehouse storage on a per-pallet and per-bin basis, billed monthly in arrears:

- Pallet location (standard): EUR 18 per pallet per month in Zaragoza; USD 22 per pallet per month in Los Angeles.
- Bin / shelf location (small SKUs): EUR 2.50 per bin per month in Zaragoza; USD 3.00 per bin per month in Los Angeles.
- Reserved dedicated aisle space: priced as a custom line item in the annual contract and is not available on standard rate cards.

Storage day count uses the higher of opening or closing occupied locations for each calendar month.

## Inbound and outbound handling fees

In addition to storage, TrackFlow applies handling fees:

- Inbound receiving: EUR 0.35 / USD 0.40 per unit for standard carton receipts.
- Pick and pack (single-item order): EUR 1.20 / USD 1.40 per order.
- Pick and pack (multi-item order): EUR 1.20 / USD 1.40 base plus EUR 0.25 / USD 0.30 per additional line.
- Returns processing (domestic, automatic path): EUR 1.50 / USD 1.70 per returned unit after warehouse receipt.

Fuel surcharges and peak-season handling uplifts, when active, are published separately in the commercial calendar and apply on top of these base rates.

## Volume tiers

Clients that average more than 500 occupied pallet locations per month across both warehouses qualify for Tier B storage rates: a 5% reduction on base pallet fees only. Bin fees and handling fees are unchanged under Tier B.

Tier C (10% pallet reduction) applies only when the annual contract commits to more than 1,000 average monthly pallet locations. Tier assignment is recalculated each quarter from measured occupancy.

## Discounts and commercial approval

No storage discount, promotional rebate, or off-rate-card reduction may be offered to a prospect or client unless Miguel Torres (Commercial Director) has approved it in writing.

Account managers must not invent percentage discounts, free storage months, or waived handling fees. If a client asks for pricing below the published rate card or outside Tier B / Tier C rules, the correct answer is that the request requires Miguel Torres's approval before any commitment is made.
