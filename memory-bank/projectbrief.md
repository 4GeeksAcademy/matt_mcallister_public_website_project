# Project Brief

## Business Description
TrackFlow is a last-mile delivery and warehouse management company founded in 2009 in Los Angeles. It operates in the United States and Spain with warehouses in Los Angeles and Zaragoza, ~130 employees, and ~€9M annual revenue.

TrackFlow stores inventory, picks and packs orders, ships through a carrier network, and handles returns for e-commerce brands in fashion, electronics, and cosmetics. For clients, logistics from order placement through delivery or return is TrackFlow's responsibility.

## Objectives
- Unify operations visibility across US and Spain.
- Replace manual carrier, returns, and reporting workflows with integrated systems.
- Deliver customer-facing and internal UIs backed by shared business logic (no duplicated rules).
- Build the TrackFlow Tech mandate: systems, integrations, and intelligent automation.

## Problem Statement
TrackFlow has strong operations but fragmented infrastructure: two warehouse systems without shared inventory, no structured carrier performance data, manual returns approval, CX agents answering repetitive questions from documents, and directors spending hours on Sunday reports. The company needs a modern, two-country logistics platform — starting with a public corporate website and an internal backoffice that surface canonical TypeScript business logic.
