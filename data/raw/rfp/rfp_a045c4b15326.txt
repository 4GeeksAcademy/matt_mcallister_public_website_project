# TrackFlow Pricing Proposal RFP

Client: Aurora Fashion Group
Submission deadline: 2026-09-15

Requested services:
- Warehousing in Los Angeles and Zaragoza
- Last-mile delivery across domestic lanes
- Returns handling and customer support

## Warehouse Operations

TrackFlow will provide inventory capacity for 10,000 units across Los Angeles and Zaragoza warehouses.
Inbound volume supports seasonal peaks with shared inventory visibility.

## Last Mile and Carriers

Carrier coverage includes UPS, FedEx, and DHL for domestic lanes.
Delivery SLA: standard ground delivery is 3-5 business days for domestic shipments.
Tracking integration is available through unified endpoints.

## Reverse Logistics

Returns process follows the TrackFlow standard return window.
Return window: 30 calendar days from delivery.
Inspection workflow uses AI-assisted condition classification.

## Customer Experience

Support channels include email, WhatsApp, and phone during business hours.
Response times target same-day acknowledgement for brand clients.
Escalation path routes unresolved cases to account managers.

## Commercial

Pricing model follows TrackFlow rate card with annual contract term.
Account management includes monthly performance reviews with Miguel Torres oversight.
